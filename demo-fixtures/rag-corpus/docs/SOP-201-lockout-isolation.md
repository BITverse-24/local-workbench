# SOP-201 — Equipment isolation and lockout

Document ID: `SOP-201`
Revision: `3.1`
Status: Approved

## [section:1.0] Purpose

This procedure defines the minimum controls for isolating equipment before
inspection, cleaning, or maintenance. It applies to mechanical, electrical,
hydraulic, and pneumatic energy sources. Isolation is complete only when the
equipment is identified, every energy source is listed, and the zero-energy
state is verified.

## [section:2.1] Identification and preparation

The person in charge must identify the equipment tag, work order, operating
unit, and isolation boundary. The planned work must state whether the item is
being inspected, repaired, cleaned, or returned to service. Associated pumps,
valves, heaters, and stored-pressure vessels must be considered even when
they are outside the immediate work area.

## [section:3.2] Lockout steps

Notify affected operators before shutdown. Stop the equipment using the normal
operating control, isolate each listed energy source, and apply a personal
lock and tag. Bleed, drain, discharge, or otherwise release stored energy.
Where practical, install a physical restraint or blind in addition to a
closed valve. Each lock and tag must identify the person who applied it.

## [section:4.1] Verification and release

Attempt a normal start or use an approved test instrument to verify that the
equipment cannot operate. Return controls to the neutral position after the
test. The permit record must include the verification method and result.
Before release, inspect the work area, remove tools, confirm that guards are
restored, and account for every person and lock.
