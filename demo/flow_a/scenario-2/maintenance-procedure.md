# Pressure Gauge Control Procedure PG-7

## 4.1 Calibration interval
Pressure gauges used for release decisions must have a current calibration certificate dated within the last 12 months. A missing or expired certificate means the gauge is not approved for release use.

## 4.2 As-found error
The as-found error must be no more than 1.0% of full scale in either direction. A gauge above this limit must be removed from service and assessed.

## 4.3 Identification
The certificate serial number must match the serial number on the gauge. A mismatch is an unresolved identity check. Do not transfer a certificate between gauges.
