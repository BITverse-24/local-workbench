# Process Safety Standard PS-14

## 6.1 Relief-valve setting
For protected equipment with a maximum allowable working pressure (MAWP) of 10.0 bar, the relief valve set pressure must be between 9.5 and 10.0 bar inclusive. A set pressure above MAWP is not compliant.

## 6.2 Bench test evidence
Each installed valve must have a recorded bench-test date within the last 12 months. If the date is absent or older than 12 months, test status is unknown and the equipment must remain out of service until verified.

## 6.3 Correction
Replace or reset any valve outside the permitted range, then record a passing bench test before release.
