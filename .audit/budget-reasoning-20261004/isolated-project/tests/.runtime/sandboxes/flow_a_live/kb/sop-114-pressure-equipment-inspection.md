# SOP-114 — Pressure Equipment Inspection and Approval

Document ID: `SOP-114`
Revision: `4.0`
Status: Approved

## [section:1.0] Purpose

This procedure defines the evidence and approval criteria for pressure
equipment inspection reports. The approval decision must identify the
equipment, preserve measured values, record defects, and state any required
repair before return to service.

## [section:2.1] Inspection evidence

Inspection findings must retain their original units, ranges, locations, and
page evidence. A report may be used for an approval note only when its
findings are traceable to the supplied inspection record.

## [section:3.2] Approval criteria

When a report identifies a tube-wall measurement below the minimum allowable
value, localised pitting, or gasket degradation, the approval note must list
each finding and its evidence. Conditional clearance may be recorded only
when the affected tubes are scheduled for retubing and any recommended gasket
replacement is recorded. The approval note must cite this section as
`[SOP-114 §3.2]`.

For the fixed E-201 report, the supported recommendation is: conditional
clearance pending retubing of the affected tubes. The note must not claim that
final approval has already been granted.

## [section:4.1] Records

The generated approval note must retain the report reference, equipment
identifier, findings summary, recommendation, and grounding references.
