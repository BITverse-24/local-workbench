# SOP-610 — Inspection instrument calibration

Document ID: `SOP-610`
Revision: `2.0`
Status: Approved

## [section:1.0] Scope

This procedure controls calibration and verification of instruments used to
produce inspection measurements. It covers identification, calibration
status, out-of-tolerance findings, and the effect of a failed instrument on
earlier measurements.

## [section:2.2] Before use

The technician must check the instrument identifier, range, resolution,
calibration status, and next-due date before taking a measurement. The
instrument must be suitable for the expected value and environmental
conditions. A missing or expired calibration label requires the instrument to
be withheld until its status is confirmed.

## [section:3.1] Out-of-tolerance result

If a calibration result is outside the approved tolerance, record the as-found
and as-left values and identify the affected range. The owner must assess
whether measurements taken since the last acceptable calibration remain valid.
That assessment must be linked to the affected inspection records and cannot
be replaced by a general statement that the instrument was usually reliable.

## [section:4.1] Calibration record

The record must include the instrument identifier, standards used, traceability
reference, environmental conditions, readings, result, technician, reviewer,
and next-due date. Calibration status supports measurement reliability but does
not itself establish an equipment approval decision.
